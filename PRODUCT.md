# Frank Kusi Appiah — personal site

Personal portfolio for Frank Kusi Appiah, Oberlin College 3-2 Engineering
student from Kumasi, Ghana. He builds robots, sensors and apps: the Oberlin
Environmental Dashboard sensor network, the Fieldline LoRaWAN platform, a
PDF reader for Even G2 glasses with 1,600+ users, and a civic AI community
calendar.

Audience: internship recruiters, engineering collaborators, and anyone
checking his work before a conversation. The page must get them to the work
in seconds, prove it with real numbers and photos, and make contact trivial
(email, GitHub, LinkedIn, resume).

Truth sources: `src/data/portfolio.ts` for projects, roles, and credentials.
Never invent metrics. All figures on the page come from that file.
